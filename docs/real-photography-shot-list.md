# PTP real-photography completion list

This list defines the authentic photographs still needed to replace the remaining representative or AI-generated imagery. Files should be supplied at their original resolution, without social-media compression, watermarks, filters or embedded text.

## Priority 1 — homepage

1. **Homepage hero — Canapa R&D and material transformation**
   - One horizontal 3:2 or 16:9 photograph, minimum 3000 px wide.
   - A real specialist working with paper, coating or material samples in the Canapa environment.
   - Leave clean negative space on the left for the headline; no confidential screens or formulas.

2. **Three technology cards**
   - DTPaper: transfer paper, printed output and textile result in one clean, unbranded setup.
   - Ceralith: coated fibre tray or paper structure in a real oven/bakery application.
   - Bioma-ORX: coated and untreated paper samples with a controlled oil/grease comparison.
   - Three horizontal 16:9 photographs, minimum 2400 × 1350 px, similar lighting and camera height.

3. **Lab-to-scale pathway — five photographs**
   - Material challenge: film, paper and fibre samples together.
   - Chemistry and substrate: formulation work with paper substrate.
   - Coating and application: real coating-bar, pilot coater or application step.
   - Validation: real measurement or controlled test, with method visible where possible.
   - Industrial scale: real paper web, coating line or production roll.
   - Five square or 4:3 photographs, minimum 1800 px on the short edge.

4. **People and integration**
   - Two or three technical specialists reviewing physical samples together.
   - Horizontal 3:2 photograph, minimum 3000 px wide.
   - Written permission from identifiable people must be available.

## Priority 2 — technology pages

5. **DTPaper process and outcome**
   - Printer output showing the translucent paper carrier and complete opaque white backing.
   - Powder unit showing the wide paper entering downward, receiving powder and returning upward.
   - Curing unit with the printed/powdered paper clearly visible.
   - Heat press and peel stage.
   - Finished dark textile with vivid, opaque colour and a close material-detail shot.
   - Four horizontal process photographs plus one finished-product hero; minimum 2400 px wide.

6. **Ceralith applications**
   - Empty coated paper/fibre structure as a material sample.
   - Real ovenable tray in an oven.
   - Bakery liner/application before and after heating.
   - Prepared-food container with no third-party brand.
   - Surface close-up after heat and grease exposure.
   - Five photographs, preferably from one controlled shoot.

7. **Bioma-ORX applications and evidence**
   - Labelled untreated and treated samples before testing.
   - Oil application during the same controlled comparison.
   - Final side-by-side observation, with test conditions recorded separately.
   - Real application examples: wrapping paper, bakery bag and fibre takeaway pack.
   - Six photographs; comparison photographs should use the same camera, light and background.

## Priority 3 — platform credibility

8. **Facility and capability**
   - Canapa exterior or entrance, laboratory wide shot, pilot/production line, roll stock and quality-control area.
   - Five horizontal photographs, minimum 3000 px wide.

9. **Material macro library**
   - Uncoated paper, coated paper, translucent DTPaper carrier, white backing layer, mineral-coated Ceralith surface and Bioma-ORX barrier surface.
   - Six macro photographs with consistent neutral lighting and a colour reference card.

## Delivery and rights checklist

- Original JPG, PNG, HEIC or RAW files; no screenshots or images downloaded from messaging apps.
- Confirm that Canapa owns the photograph or has written commercial website permission.
- Avoid visible customer brands, machinery serial numbers, confidential labels and computer screens.
- Identify technology, process stage, date, location, photographer and permission owner for every file.
- Provide both horizontal and vertical framing when possible; do not add logos or text before delivery.
- Web stock photography may be used only for non-proprietary context after its licence and any visible trademarks or people are checked.
